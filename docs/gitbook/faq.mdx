## Why Does Iruka Start With K-pop Instead Of TCG?

Iruka is built for physical fandom collectibles rather than gameplay-led
cards. K-pop photocards are identified by artist, member, release, event or
benefit, official source, condition, and grade. Iruka keeps that identity
connected to transparent packs, verification, custody, trading, and physical
Redemption.

K-pop is the initial category because published consumer and commerce data
shows meaningful demand for physical fandom goods. See
[Why Fandom Collectibles](/project/why-fandom-collectibles).

## Do All Photocards Gain Value?

No. Category demand does not guarantee that an individual photocard will
appreciate or remain liquid. Official issuance, exact variant, member demand,
distribution scarcity, condition, grade, and comparable completed sales can
all affect observed value.

Iruka will omit an estimate when sufficiently matched market evidence is not
available. Any displayed estimate is dated reference data, not a return or
repurchase promise.

## Does Every Card Need Professional Grading?

No. Professional grading adds cost and turnaround time and may not be
economical for an accessible card. Iruka plans a documented identity,
authentication, condition, photography, and custody track for eligible
lower-price inventory.

Eligible premium inventory may use certificate-verifiable third-party grading
in addition to the same physical intake and custody controls.

## How Does Iruka Expand Beyond K-pop?

Iruka plans to reuse the same Vending, Vault, Marketplace, and Redemption
infrastructure for officially issued trot, cheerleader, and sports
collectibles. Each category launches only after its physical supply,
authentication, grading acceptance, metadata, value evidence, storage, and
shipping controls are documented.

New artwork or branded drops remain a separate licensed-IP process.

## Do I Need A Wallet?

No wallet extension is required to browse or sign in. Privy assigns every
signed-in account a user-controlled Iruka Wallet, whether the collector uses
email, Google, MetaMask, or another supported EVM login method. The Iruka
Wallet submits GIWA Sepolia test pulls.

An external wallet is optional. It can fund the Iruka Wallet or receive a
same-chain withdrawal. Current transfers use GIWA Sepolia test ETH only;
commercial payment support is planned and will be documented separately.

## Are Iruka Cards Authentic?

No commercial inventory is live today. Before launch, Iruka intends to list
only physical cards that have completed the applicable authentication,
photography, intake, and custody checks. A card is not marked `Vaulted` from a
seller claim or certificate number alone.

## How Can I Verify Pack Odds?

Each production batch will publish its inventory snapshot, total supply,
individual card odds, rarity-tier odds, and GIWA commitments. The current
testnet contract records the batch root, odds commitment, draw-seed root,
requests, fulfillments, and remaining supply.

The testnet design is commit-reveal, not VRF. Its limitations are documented in
[Onchain Architecture](/technical/onchain-architecture).

## Is Rarity The Same As A Grade?

No. `Common`, `Rare`, `Epic`, `Legendary`, and `Iruka` describe allocation
within a pack. A physical grade describes card condition under an
authentication or grading process. One label cannot substitute for the other.

## Which Grading Companies Does Iruka Use?

BRG and CGC are under evaluation for eligible premium inventory. Iruka has not
announced a partnership with either company. Lower-price inventory may use
Iruka's documented authentication and condition-record track instead of
third-party grading.

See [Grading and Verification](/product/grading-and-verification).

## What Does GIWA Record?

The current GIWA Sepolia contract records pack-batch commitments, pull
requests, fulfillment proofs, assigned inventory commitments, and remaining
supply. Card images, grading evidence, storage locations, personal data, and
shipping addresses stay offchain.

The current contract does not mint ERC-721 ownership or settle USDC.

## Can I Receive The Physical Card?

Not yet. Redemption and shipping are planned. Before launch, Iruka will publish
supported regions, identity and address checks, fees, handling targets,
tracking, and loss or damage procedures.

## Are Packs Official Artist Or Agency Collaborations?

Not unless Iruka publishes a signed collaboration announcement. Resale records
may use names only to identify genuine physical items. New artwork, branded
packs, or licensed drops require rights-holder approval before release.

## Is Estimated Value Guaranteed?

No. Any future estimated value will be a dated reference based on disclosed
market evidence. It is not a guaranteed sale price, return, or repurchase
offer.
