> **Status: MVP Preview** - The interface can be explored today. The payment, custody, and GIWA settlement steps below are the launch specification.

## 1. Sign In

Collectors will be able to sign in with email or Google through a Privy embedded EVM wallet. MetaMask and OKX Wallet are the initial external wallet targets.

## 2. Choose a Pack

Before purchase, a pack must show its USDC price, total and remaining supply, complete card pool, individual odds, value methodology, and redemption eligibility.

## 3. Pull and Reveal

The collector confirms a USDC purchase. The production draw will use a commit-reveal process recorded on GIWA so the selected inventory item can be independently checked after reveal.

The result shows the card image, pack rarity, BRG grade, inventory ID, estimated value evidence, and ownership transaction.

## 4. Keep It Vaulted

The physical card stays with Iruka while its 1:1 ERC-721 represents ownership. Vaulted cards can be transferred to another EVM wallet or listed for a fixed USDC price.

## 5. Sell or Ship

The owner can sell the vaulted card through the Marketplace or request delivery in South Korea. A listed card must be delisted before shipping can begin.

## Wallet and Gas

Iruka plans to sponsor gas for in-product actions such as pack purchases and Marketplace settlement. Users pay the network fee when transferring assets to an external wallet.

USDC transactions will be enabled only after a canonical GIWA deployment and contract address are verified. No unofficial bridged token is presented as the launch settlement asset.
