Iruka brings the collectible journey into one continuous product:

**Vending → Reveal → Vault → Marketplace → Redemption**

K-pop photocards are the initial category. The same lifecycle is designed to
support officially issued trot, cheerleader, and sports collectibles only
after each category passes its own sourcing and verification gates.

## The Collector Journey

### 1. Vending

Collectors browse without signing in, compare the available pack tier, and
review the eligible card pool and published odds. A production pack must also
show the inventory snapshot, remaining supply, value methodology, and physical
redemption status before purchase.

The current Debut pack is a GIWA Sepolia test batch. Stage, Encore, and Grail
remain **Coming soon**.

### 2. Reveal

After sign-in, one Pull reserves a position from the selected batch. The result
is revealed only after Iruka receives the assigned inventory record. A GIWA
pack also shows the request and fulfillment receipts that produced that
result.

Reveal presents the assigned card; it does not choose the result. A delayed
fulfillment resumes from the same request instead of creating another paid
Pull.

### 3. Vault

A production card enters the Vault only after the exact physical item is
received, authenticated, photographed, assigned a storage record, and matched
to its ownership and redemption records.

A certificate number, image, test Pull, or account inventory row is not enough
to claim physical custody.

### 4. Marketplace

Only eligible vaulted inventory can enter the Marketplace. One listing points
to one `inventoryId`, and listing the card locks it against physical
redemption. A completed sale transfers the ownership record while the same
physical item remains in custody.

### 5. Redemption

Redemption removes an active listing, locks the matching physical item, checks
the destination and applicable terms, and releases that exact card through the
published fulfillment process.

Personal identity, address, carrier, and storage-location data remain offchain.

## Account And Wallet

Email, Google, and supported EVM wallet login methods create an Iruka account
through Privy. Each signed-in account receives a user-controlled Iruka Wallet
on EVM, including accounts that use MetaMask or another external wallet to sign
in.

The Iruka Wallet submits the GIWA Sepolia Pull. An external wallet is optional
and remains separate: it can fund the Iruka Wallet or receive a same-chain
withdrawal.

Current Add funds and Withdraw actions transfer GIWA Sepolia test ETH only.
They are not fiat funding, cross-chain routing, or a GIWA-to-Ethereum bridge.
Testnet ETH has no monetary value, and Iruka does not currently accept
commercial USDC payments.

## Product State Rules

Iruka's commercial flow is designed to fail closed:

- A Pull result does not automatically prove physical custody.
- An unverified item cannot become `Vaulted`.
- A non-vaulted item cannot be listed.
- A listed item must be delisted before Redemption.
- A redeemed item cannot return to inventory without a new intake.
