---
title: "IP and Listing Policy"
description: "Follow Iruka rules for neutral pack branding, marketplace identification, and licensed assets."
icon: "shield-check"
---

Iruka should keep public pack branding neutral unless the team has rights, licenses, or partnership approval.

## Neutral Public Branding

Use neutral pack names such as:

- Girl Group Iruka Pack
- Boy Group Iruka Pack
- Premium Idol Drop
- Rookie Idol Drop
- TCG Slab Pack

Avoid using third-party artist names, agency names, group names, game names, logos, or official images as Iruka-owned pack branding without permission.

## Marketplace Identification

Marketplace listings and vault item details may use names that are needed to identify a genuine physical item.

Use this narrowly:

- Item title
- Set or album name
- Member or character name
- Condition
- Grade
- Serial or card number

## Disclaimer

Iruka is not affiliated with artists, agencies, labels, publishers, card manufacturers, or grading companies unless explicitly stated.

## Asset Rule

Use original Iruka pack art, neutral mockups, or photos of actual inventory. Do not create commercial cards using third-party faces, logos, or artwork without rights.
