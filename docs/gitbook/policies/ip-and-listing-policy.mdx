## Availability Labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| MVP Preview | The interface can be explored, but real settlement or custody is unavailable |
| Planned | The behavior is part of the launch specification but is not operational |
| Live | The feature has operating records and can be used with real assets |

Iruka uses `Live` only when the relevant inventory, contract, payment, custody, or fulfillment evidence exists.

## Authentic Resale Inventory

Initial inventory is limited to genuine photocards purchased by Iruka. Public pack names stay neutral unless Iruka has a signed license or partnership covering the use.

Marketplace and Vault records may use a group, member, album, set, or manufacturer name only when needed to identify an actual physical card. This descriptive use must not imply sponsorship or affiliation.

Public imagery uses original Iruka art or photographs of the exact inventory. Iruka does not create commercial card art from unlicensed artist faces, logos, official photographs, or brand assets.

## Authentication

Iruka plans to require professional BRG authentication for every circulating launch card. Each record is intended to include a certificate ID, grade, front and back images, and Iruka inventory ID. This does not state that a BRG partnership currently exists.

Pack rarity is an allocation tier and must not be presented as a substitute for the physical grade.

## Collector Safeguards

- Product viewing is open, but pack purchases, Marketplace trading, and redemption are limited to verified users aged 19 or older at launch.
- A pack can be cancelled before allocation begins. After allocation, simple change-of-mind refunds are not offered; duplicate charges, incorrect allocation, service errors, defects, and statutory rights remain covered.
- Estimated values are reference data, not guaranteed sale prices.
- Iruka does not promise buybacks, investment returns, or a minimum resale value.
- Insurance is not described as active until the provider and coverage terms are published.

Before commercial launch, Iruka must publish the verified operator identity, Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, refund terms, and shipping terms after legal review.
