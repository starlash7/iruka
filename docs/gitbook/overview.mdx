> **Status: MVP Preview** - The current app demonstrates the product flow. A GIWA Sepolia testnet contract path is deployment-ready; it is not commercial payment, custody, or onchain ownership until the verified contract address is published.

Iruka connects K-pop photocard packs, verified physical custody, marketplace trading, and physical redemption in one product.

## The Collecting Loop

1. Choose a pack with published supply, contents, and odds.
2. Pull and reveal one authenticated photocard.
3. Keep the physical card in the Iruka Vault.
4. Sell or transfer its digital ownership, or ship the card to Korea.

## What Ownership Means

At launch, each eligible card will be backed by one physical photocard held by Iruka and one ERC-721 ownership record on GIWA. The token records who owns the vaulted card. It does not replace physical intake, authentication, or custody records.

## Launch Scope

- Photocards purchased and stored by Iruka
- Professional grading and certification planned through BRG
- Canonical USDC payments on GIWA after official support is verified
- Fixed-price marketplace listings
- Free storage while a card remains vaulted
- Physical redemption in South Korea first

No commercial pack is live until its physical inventory, certification records, odds, payment asset, and redemption eligibility are verified.
