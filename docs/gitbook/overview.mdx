## Product Vision

Iruka is Korea-first infrastructure for physical fandom collectibles, starting
with K-pop photocards. It is designed to extend the same verified product rails
to officially issued trot, cheerleader, and sports collectibles.

Unlike a gameplay-led TCG catalog, a fandom collectible is identified by its
artist or team, member or player, release, event or benefit, official source,
condition, and grade. Iruka preserves that identity through one product:

**Vending → Reveal → Vault → Marketplace → Redemption**

The goal is not to turn an image into a collectible. It is to connect one
genuine physical item to transparent pack rules, verification, controlled
custody, trading, and physical delivery.

## Why K-pop First

K-pop already has a global, collection-driven physical merchandise market.
The [Korea Consumer Agency](https://www.kca.go.kr/eng/sub.do?menukey=6007&mode=view&no=1003533572&page=3)
found that **52.7%** of surveyed K-pop fans had purchased albums to collect
fandom goods. [Weverse](https://en.weverse.co/news/?bmode=view&idx=165565023)
reported that its physical merchandise sales, including albums and
collectibles, grew **10% year over year** in 2024.

These signals support K-pop as Iruka's first category. They do not show that
every photocard will rise in value. Each card still requires exact identity,
condition, verification, custody, and completed-sale evidence.

See [Why Fandom Collectibles](/project/why-fandom-collectibles) for the market
sources, category model, and claim boundaries.

## Core Product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Vending">
    Select a pack and review its tier, eligible card pool, supply, odds, and
    redemption status before opening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reveal">
    Pull one committed position and reveal the assigned card with a transaction
    receipt when the pack runs on GIWA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Vault">
    Connect the digital record to the same physical item only after intake,
    authentication, custody, and ownership records agree.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Marketplace">
    List an eligible vaulted card at a fixed price while the physical item
    remains secured under the same inventory record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Redemption">
    Delist and lock the item, verify the destination, and release the matching
    physical card through the published fulfillment process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [How Iruka Works](/project/how-it-works) for the complete collector journey
and the current availability of each step.

## Business Direction

Iruka starts with verified physical inventory because product trust must be
proven before new IP is created around it.

1. **Verified physical inventory** - establish sourcing, authentication,
   grading, Vault, marketplace, and redemption operations for genuine,
   officially issued cards.
2. **Licensed IP drops** - create new cards and branded pack experiences only
   after rights holders approve the assets, product scope, territory, and
   distribution.
3. **Creator experiences** - give approved creators controlled tools for
   original collectible releases.
4. **Iruka Universe** - connect collection identity with moderated fan and
   collector communities.

This path expands the product without treating an artist name, image, or fan
community as permission to create commercial merchandise. See
[Roadmap](/roadmap) for the release gates and category expansion plan.

## Why GIWA

Iruka is a Korea-first consumer and RWA product. GIWA provides an
EVM-compatible environment, public transaction verification, and approximately
one-second blocks without requiring Iruka to create a separate wallet standard
or contract toolchain.

The current product uses GIWA Sepolia for pack commitments and Pull receipts.
Future GIWA wallet and paymaster capabilities remain ecosystem direction, not
features Iruka claims as live today.

- [GIWA network overview](https://docs.giwa.io/giwa-chain/en)
- [GIWA and Ethereum compatibility](https://docs.giwa.io/giwa-chain/en/network-information/diffs-ethereum-giwa)

## Execution Evidence

Iruka separates product claims by how they can be checked.

| Claim | Current evidence | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pack rules exist before a Pull | Debut supply, inventory root, odds commitment, and draw-seed root were recorded in a `BatchCommitted` transaction | Mined on GIWA Sepolia |
| A fulfilled Pull consumes one committed position | `PullRequested` and `PullFulfilled` receipts reference the deployed contract and assigned inventory commitment | Mined on GIWA Sepolia |
| A delayed result resumes without a second Pull | The browser persists the request ID and scheduled Keeper recovery fulfills the same request | Reproducible in the product |
| A card is physically held and redeemable | Intake, verification, custody, and ownership records must agree | In validation |

The immutable deployment snapshot is:

| Item | Published value |
| --- | ---: |
| Source-verified pack contracts | 1 |
| Committed Debut test positions | 100 |
| Initial rarity allocation | 10,000 basis points |
| Documented request and fulfillment reference pairs | 1 |

These are technical execution records, not user, revenue, custody, or trading
metrics. Contract addresses and transaction links are published in
[GIWA Contracts](/technical/giwa-testnet).
