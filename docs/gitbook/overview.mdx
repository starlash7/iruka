Iruka is building one collecting flow for discovering physical photocard packs,
revealing a card, holding verified inventory in a Vault, trading eligible cards,
and requesting physical redemption.

The public application currently demonstrates this flow and records test pulls
on GIWA Sepolia. Commercial packs, custody, marketplace settlement, and
shipping are not live.

## The Collecting Loop

1. **Sign in** with email, Google, or a supported EVM wallet.
2. **Select a pack** and review its inventory, supply, odds, value snapshot, and
   redemption eligibility.
3. **Pull** one card from the committed batch.
4. **Reveal** the assigned inventory record and transaction receipt.
5. **Vault** the card only after physical intake, verification, and custody
   records agree.
6. **Sell or redeem** an eligible vaulted card.

The GIWA Sepolia application currently covers the testnet portion of steps
1-4. Steps 5-6 remain in validation or planned until the required physical and
commercial operations are available.

## Operating Standard

Iruka treats product claims as records, not labels:

- Pack odds must match a published inventory snapshot and onchain commitment.
- Rarity and physical grade must remain separate.
- A certificate lookup alone cannot establish custody.
- A listing must reference one verified physical item.
- A listed item must be removed from sale before redemption begins.
- Estimated value is reference data, not a guaranteed sale price.
