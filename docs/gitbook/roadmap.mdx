> **Current stage: MVP Preview** - Roadmap stages advance through verified release gates, not uncommitted calendar dates.

## 1. Iruka Vending

Build the first complete photocard collecting loop.

Release gates:

- Iruka-owned inventory authenticated and graded
- Canonical USDC and EVM wallet flow verified on GIWA
- 1:1 ownership, commit-reveal, and fixed-price settlement contracts ready
- Korean custody, legal, and five-business-day shipping operations ready
- First production batch published with full inventory and odds

## 2. Licensed IP Drops

Open official drops only after rights are secured.

Release gates:

- Signed agreement defining IP, territory, duration, and sales rights
- Agency or rights-holder approval for artwork and campaign copy
- Drop-specific inventory, odds, and fulfillment terms published

## 3. Creator Playground

Give approved creators a controlled way to design original pack experiences.

Release gates:

- Creator identity, ownership, and content-rights review
- Pack approval, payout, and reporting rules
- Original-asset and prohibited-content enforcement

## 4. The Iruka Universe

Connect collecting with fan communities and collection identity.

Release gates:

- Collection profiles and community boards
- Reporting, moderation, privacy, and account-safety controls
- Clear separation between licensed, original, and resale inventory

A stage is marked `Live` only after its release gates have public operating evidence.
