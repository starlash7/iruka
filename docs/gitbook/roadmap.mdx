## 1. Iruka Vending

Deliver the complete physical photocard loop from published pack inventory to
verified custody, trading, and redemption.

Release gates:

- Documented physical sourcing and inventory ownership
- Authentication, grading, photography, intake, and custody operations
- Published batch supply, card pool, odds, and value evidence
- Audited production randomness and one-to-one ownership contracts
- Canonical USDC payment and fixed-price marketplace settlement
- Redemption, shipping, support, privacy, and legal policies

## 2. Licensed IP Drops

Launch newly designed cards or branded pack experiences only after the
necessary rights are secured.

Release gates:

- Signed rights holder agreement covering assets, product, territory, and term
- Approval process for artwork, names, campaign copy, and distribution
- Drop-specific inventory, odds, settlement, custody, and fulfillment terms
- Rights reporting and takedown procedures

## 3. Creator Playground

Give approved creators a controlled path to original collectible experiences.

Release gates:

- Creator identity and content-rights review
- Original-asset and prohibited-content enforcement
- Pack approval, payout, reporting, and dispute rules
- Separation from third-party resale inventory

## 4. The Iruka Universe

Connect collection identity with moderated fan and collector communities.

Release gates:

- Collection profiles and community boards
- Reporting, moderation, privacy, and account-safety controls
- Clear labels for resale, original, and licensed inventory
- Community operations and incident response

## Category Expansion Track

Category expansion begins with genuine, officially issued physical inventory.
It does not require Iruka to create new artist or team imagery.

| Order | Category | Initial operating goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | K-pop photocards | Validate sourcing, verification, grading, Vault, marketplace, and redemption operations |
| 2 | Trot photocards | Add officially issued physical cards after the same operating gates pass |
| 3 | Cheerleader photocards | Add officially issued cards with documented identity and supply |
| 4 | Sports player cards | Expand to athlete cards and domestic sports collectibles with category-specific verification |

Each category launches independently only after Iruka can document:

- Physical supply source and acquisition records
- Authenticity and item-identification method
- Third-party grader acceptance where applicable
- Intake, storage, reconciliation, and redemption controls
- Comparable-sales data and value methodology
- Packaging, delivery, support, and regional rules

Licensed drops that create new images, cards, or branded experiences follow the
separate rights holder process in Stage 2.

A stage or category is marked `Live` only after its release gates have public
contracts, policies, or operating evidence.
