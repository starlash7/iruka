## Product Direction

Iruka starts by proving the complete operating loop for genuine physical
collectibles. Once sourcing, verification, custody, trading, and redemption
work as one system, the same foundation can support licensed releases, creator
tools, and community experiences.

The roadmap therefore expands in this order:

**Iruka Vending → Licensed IP Drops → Creator Playground → The Iruka Universe**

Each stage must produce public contracts, policies, or operating records before
its status changes to `Live`.

## 1. Iruka Vending

Deliver the complete physical photocard loop from published pack inventory to
verified custody, trading, and redemption.

Collector outcome:

- Open a pack with visible inventory and odds.
- Confirm the assigned result and ownership record.
- Keep the physical card in the Vault, sell it, or request Redemption.

Release gates:

- Documented physical sourcing and inventory ownership
- Authentication, grading, photography, intake, and custody operations
- Published batch supply, card pool, odds, and value evidence
- Audited production randomness and one-to-one ownership contracts
- Canonical USDC payment and fixed-price Marketplace settlement
- Redemption, shipping, support, privacy, and legal policies

## 2. Licensed IP Drops

Launch newly designed cards or branded pack experiences only after the
necessary rights are secured.

Collector outcome:

- Access officially approved cards and pack experiences unavailable through
  ordinary resale inventory.
- See drop-specific supply, odds, ownership, and fulfillment terms before
  purchase.

Release gates:

- Signed rights holder agreement covering assets, product, territory, and term
- Approval process for artwork, names, campaign copy, and distribution
- Drop-specific inventory, odds, settlement, custody, and fulfillment terms
- Rights reporting and takedown procedures

## 3. Creator Playground

Give approved creators a controlled path to original collectible experiences.

Collector outcome:

- Discover original releases with clearly identified creators and rights.
- Collect across the same Vending, Vault, Marketplace, and Redemption flow.

Release gates:

- Creator identity and content-rights review
- Original-asset and prohibited-content enforcement
- Pack approval, payout, reporting, and dispute rules
- Separation from third-party resale inventory

## 4. The Iruka Universe

Connect collection identity with moderated fan and collector communities.

Collector outcome:

- Build a collection profile around owned items.
- Join boards and events connected to approved collectible categories.

Release gates:

- Collection profiles and community boards
- Reporting, moderation, privacy, and account-safety controls
- Clear labels for resale, original, and licensed inventory
- Community operations and incident response

## Category Expansion Track

Category expansion begins with genuine, officially issued physical inventory.
It does not require Iruka to create new artist or team imagery. It reuses the
same physical collectible infrastructure while adding the identity, sourcing,
verification, valuation, and fulfillment rules required by each category.

| Order | Category | Initial operating goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | K-pop photocards | Prove the initial fandom-native catalog and validate sourcing, verification, grading, Vault, Marketplace, and Redemption operations |
| 2 | Trot photocards | Add officially issued physical cards after the same operating gates pass |
| 3 | Cheerleader photocards | Add officially issued cards with documented identity and supply |
| 4 | Sports player cards | Expand to athlete cards and domestic sports collectibles with category-specific verification |

Each category launches independently only after Iruka can document:

- Physical supply source and acquisition records
- Authenticity and item-identification method
- Third-party grader acceptance where applicable
- Intake, storage, reconciliation, and redemption controls
- Comparable-sales data and value methodology
- Packaging, delivery, support, and regional rules

Licensed drops that create new images, cards, or branded experiences follow the
separate rights holder process in Stage 2.

## Product Validation Metrics

Iruka will publish measured values after private-pilot instrumentation begins.
It will not substitute targets or sample transactions for user traction.

| Product area | Measurement |
| --- | --- |
| Vending | Sign-in-to-Pull completion and request confirmation rate |
| Testnet fulfillment, measured separately by network | Request-to-fulfillment P50/P95 and pending-request recovery rate |
| Reveal | Completed Reveal rate and explicit Skip rate |
| Vault | Verification completion and custody-record reconciliation |
| Marketplace | Eligible listing creation and completed settlement |
| Redemption | Delist-to-lock success and completed physical fulfillment |

Contract invariants remain non-negotiable regardless of volume: published odds
must reconcile, one committed position cannot be fulfilled twice, only complete
custody records can become `Vaulted`, and a listed item must be delisted before
Redemption.
