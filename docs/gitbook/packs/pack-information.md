## Pack Naming Rule

Pack names should not use third-party artist, agency, label, game, or brand names as Iruka-owned product branding unless Iruka has the appropriate rights or partnership.

Use neutral names for public drops. Use specific item names only inside actual marketplace listing details, vault item details, or inventory records where they identify a genuine physical product.

## Pack Lifecycle

1. Inventory is sourced and verified.
2. Items are grouped into a pack pool.
3. Pack price, odds, and value bands are published.
4. Users pull packs until supply is exhausted.
5. Revealed items move into vault, marketplace, or shipping flows.
