> **Status: Live testnet behavior** - The reveal timeline and GIWA receipt
> checks are implemented. Commercial inventory, custody, and redemption remain
> in validation or planned.

Iruka reveals are part of Vending. A completed pull moves through one short
sequence and ends on a result that the collector can keep in Vault, list, or
ship when redemption is eligible.

## State Machine

```text
idle -> charging -> tear -> reveal -> summary
```

- `idle`: the selected pack remains visible before the Pull action.
- `charging`: no more than 500ms of anticipation.
- `tear`: play a configured pack-opening clip or the CSS pack tear.
- `reveal`: show the resulting card as interactive DOM.
- `summary`: show rarity, serial, value range, and verified receipt when one exists.

The summary stays open until the collector continues. A result is not added to
the current session before summary is reached.

## Rarity Pacing

| Tier | Maximum | Treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Common | 1.5s | Fast transition and restrained foil |
| Rare | 3.0s | Green flare and moderate foil |
| Epic | 3.0s | Red flare and stronger foil |
| Legendary | 4.5s | Yellow flare and premium sequence |
| Iruka | 4.5s | Holographic blue and strongest sequence |

Later reveals use the quick timeline. Skip moves directly to summary from
every active state. Legendary and Iruka may hold briefly before the final card
appears, but the full sequence must stay within 4.5 seconds.

## Media Contract

- Mobile tear clips use `9:16`.
- Desktop clips use `1:1` or `16:9`.
- Clips use a black compositing background and end on a light flash.
- Clips contain no text, watermark, third-party logo, artist, or real hand.
- Original Iruka pack art is the image-to-video source.
- Video is preloaded in the overlay before the tear state.
- A missing or failed clip immediately falls back to CSS.

Store approved assets at:

```text
public/videos/pack-{common,rare,premium}-*
public/sfx/{tear,whoosh,sparkle,rare-sting}.*
```

No placeholder video or audio is generated for the review build.

## DOM Card

The revealed card uses regular DOM:

- front and branded back faces
- pointer tilt after reveal
- glare position derived from the pointer
- click or tap to flip
- rarity color from the shared reveal configuration

Pointer movement updates CSS variables directly so it does not trigger React
renders. Reduced-motion disables tilt, glare, flashing, and long transitions.

## Sound And Accessibility

- Sound is generic and muted by default.
- A visible control reports whether sound is on or off.
- Escape skips an active sequence and closes a completed summary.
- The card remains keyboard focusable.
- The CSS fallback does not require WebGL.
- Missing storage, media, audio, or haptic support cannot block the result.

## GIWA Evidence

The browser may show a GIWA Sepolia Explorer link only when it has a real
transaction hash. A request receipt proves that a draw position was reserved;
the assigned card is proven only after `PullFulfilled`.

For a configured GIWA batch, Reveal remains closed until the browser reads the
fulfilled request, finds its canonical `PullFulfilled` transaction, and
matches the returned inventory commitment to the local card at that inventory
index. A pending request is checked again without submitting a second pull.

After the collector's `requestPull` transaction confirms, the browser asks the
same-origin Keeper endpoint to submit the committed server-seed and inventory
proof. The collector does not sign a fulfillment transaction. Keeper retries
remain tied to the original request ID, and the contract event remains the
source of truth if the endpoint is delayed or temporarily unavailable.

The local fixture flow may demonstrate reveal motion, but it must not display
a fabricated contract address, request identifier, block number, or Explorer
URL.

## Source Boundaries

```text
src/features/pack-reveal/
  PackRevealOverlay.tsx
  RevealCard.tsx
  RevealTear.tsx
  revealMachine.ts
  revealConfig.ts
  sounds.ts
  useRevealTimeline.ts
```

`revealMachine.ts` owns pure phase timing. `useRevealTimeline.ts` owns browser
timers and skip. `RevealTear.tsx` owns optional media failure. `RevealCard.tsx`
owns tilt, glare, and flip. `PackRevealOverlay.tsx` composes the dialog and
summary.

## Acceptance

- State order matches the sprint state machine.
- Charging is at most 500ms.
- Rarity maximums are enforced.
- Skip works from every active phase.
- Sound is muted by default.
- Reduced-motion completes through the short CSS path.
- Missing video does not block reveal.
- The result persists until the collector continues.
- A configured GIWA pull reveals only the inventory committed by
  `PullFulfilled`.
- Explorer links are backed by real GIWA Sepolia transaction hashes.
