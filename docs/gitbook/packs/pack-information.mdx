> **Status: Planned** - The current app uses preview pack data. Commercial pack details will be published only after physical inventory is certified and secured.

Iruka launches with authenticated K-pop photocards sourced and owned by Iruka. Albums, apparel, and other merchandise are not part of the first production pack scope.

## What Every Pack Publishes

- Batch ID and inventory snapshot time
- USDC price, total supply, and remaining supply
- Front and back images for every eligible card
- Iruka inventory ID, BRG certificate, and grade for each card
- Individual card odds and rarity-tier odds
- Estimated value evidence and snapshot time
- Physical redemption eligibility
- GIWA commitment transaction

Cards are drawn without replacement. Displayed odds are calculated from the eligible cards remaining at the published snapshot. A material pool change requires a new batch version rather than a silent edit.

## Rarities and Odds

| Tier | Color | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | Gray | Baseline allocation tier |
| Rare | Green | Scarcer or higher-value tier |
| Epic | Red | High-value chase tier |
| Legendary | Yellow | Very limited premium tier |
| Iruka | Holographic blue | Highest chase tier |

Pack rarity describes allocation within a specific batch. It is separate from the physical grade assigned by BRG.

## Estimated Value

Iruka's planned estimate uses verified completed sales from the previous 90 days for the same card and a comparable grade. It uses up to 10 recent sales and publishes the median, range, sample count, and snapshot time.

At least three comparable sales are required to show a median. With fewer records, the card is marked `Limited data` instead of receiving a guaranteed value.

## Verifiable Draws

1. Verified ERC-721 inventory is deposited into a batch contract.
2. The inventory root and server-seed commitment are recorded on GIWA before sales.
3. A purchase creates a draw request tied to the current batch state.
4. Iruka reveals the committed seed and selected inventory ID.
5. The collector receives a receipt linking the batch, card, token, and GIWA transaction.

The current browser-based random result is an interface preview and is not the production allocation method.
