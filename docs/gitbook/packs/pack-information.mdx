## Pack Model

Every Iruka pack has two independent labels:

- **Category** identifies the fandom collectible market and its required
  metadata.
- **Tier** describes the collector experience and intended inventory profile.

| Category | Current state | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **K-pop** | Testnet | Initial category for proving photocard identity, verification, Vault, Marketplace, and Redemption |
| **Trot** | Planned | Officially issued trot photocards after category-specific supply and identity checks |
| **Cheerleader** | Planned | Officially issued cards with team, season, event, and subject records |
| **Sports** | Planned | Player cards and domestic sports collectibles with category-specific grading evidence |

The category does not determine price or rarity. The same category may contain
different tiers after its inventory and operating controls are ready.

| Tier | Product role | Intended inventory |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Debut** | Entry pack for collectors opening Iruka for the first time | Accessible verified photocards with a clear, broad card pool |
| **Stage** | Balanced pack for repeat collectors | A balanced mix of accessible cards and scarcer chase inventory |
| **Encore** | Premium pack with a narrower card pool | Higher concentration of premium authenticated or graded inventory |
| **Grail** | Highest Iruka tier with limited availability | Scarce, high-confidence graded cards with complete verification records |

The tier names do not promise a return or specific grade. Every commercial
batch publishes its actual inventory and odds independently.

## Pack Publication Standard

Every commercial pack must publish the following before sales open:

| Field | Required disclosure |
| --- | --- |
| Batch | Batch ID, version, status, and inventory snapshot time |
| Supply | Original supply, eligible supply, and remaining supply |
| Inventory | Complete card pool with one unique inventory ID per physical item |
| Odds | Individual card probability and aggregate rarity probability |
| Value | Evidence source, comparable set, range, and snapshot time |
| Redemption | Eligibility and any item-specific restriction |
| GIWA | Contract, batch commitment, and transaction link |

Cards are assigned without replacement. Displayed odds must be recalculated
from the eligible inventory remaining at the published snapshot. Removing,
adding, or replacing inventory requires a new batch version and commitment.

## Fandom Inventory Identity

One physical card keeps the same `inventoryId` across its pack, Vault,
Marketplace, and Redemption records. The category-specific identity fields are:

| Field | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `category` | K-pop, trot, cheerleader, or sports |
| `artistOrTeam` | Officially identified artist, group, club, or team |
| `memberOrPlayer` | Member, cheerleader, athlete, or other depicted subject |
| `release` | Album, season, event, tour, set, or product release |
| `eventOrBenefit` | Distribution event, preorder benefit, or other official variant |
| `cardType` | Album inclusion, benefit, merchandise, graded slab, or licensed release |
| `officialSource` | Issuer and documented acquisition source |
| `verificationTrack` | Iruka verification or eligible third-party grading |

These fields identify genuine physical inventory. They do not imply that Iruka
is affiliated with the named artist, agency, team, issuer, or marketplace.

## Rarities and Odds

| Rarity | Test positions | Initial probability | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Common | 60 | 60% | Baseline allocation tier |
| Rare | 28 | 28% | Scarcer allocation tier |
| Epic | 9 | 9% | Premium chase allocation |
| Legendary | 2 | 2% | Very limited allocation |
| Iruka | 1 | 1% | Highest chase allocation |

These values total 100% at the initial snapshot. Probabilities change as
positions are fulfilled because the batch draws without replacement.

Pack rarity is not a physical grade. Rarity describes allocation inside one
batch. Grade describes the condition and verification outcome of a physical
card.

## Individual Card Odds

In a commercial batch, each eligible physical item receives one inventory
position and one published probability. The current test fixture instead uses
100 weighted positions across 20 preview identities to validate the product
and contract flow.

Iruka does not use a hidden replacement pool. A material correction pauses the
affected commercial batch and requires a new public snapshot.

## Estimated Value

Estimated value is **In validation**. Before values appear on a commercial
pack, Iruka will publish:

- Exact card identity and comparable condition or grade
- Completed-sale sources and observation window
- Currency and conversion timestamp
- Sample count, median, and observed range
- Snapshot time and limited-data handling

Listings and asking prices are not completed sales. An estimate is reference
data and never guarantees a sale price, return, or future liquidity.

## GIWA Commitment

Before a test Pull, Iruka records the inventory root, odds commitment, and
draw-seed root on GIWA Sepolia. A collector request reserves a draw index. The
Keeper later proves the committed draw seed and selected inventory membership.

See [Onchain Architecture](/technical/onchain-architecture) for what this
contract proves and what remains offchain.
