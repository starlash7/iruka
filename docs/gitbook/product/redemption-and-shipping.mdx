Redemption converts a vaulted digital ownership record into delivery of its exact physical photocard.

## Redemption Flow

1. The owner delists the card if it is for sale.
2. Iruka verifies the connected wallet, age, custody record, and shipping eligibility.
3. The owner confirms the Korean address and pays the displayed shipping and packaging cost.
4. The ERC-721 is locked so it cannot be sold or transferred.
5. Iruka prepares the exact inventory item for shipment.
6. At carrier handoff, the token is burned and tracking is attached to the record.

The request can be cancelled before packing begins. If fulfillment fails before carrier handoff, the token is unlocked and any refundable shipping charge is returned.

## Cost and Timing

- Vault storage is free.
- The owner pays the displayed domestic shipping and secure packaging cost.
- Iruka dispatches within five business days after identity, ownership, address, and payment checks are complete.
- Carrier delivery time begins after dispatch and is shown separately.

## Status Model

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Redemption locked | Ownership and inventory are reserved for shipment |
| Preparing | Item is being packed |
| Shipped | Carrier has the item and the token is retired |
| Delivered | Delivery confirmed |
| Exception | Address, payment, inventory, or carrier review is required |

International shipping remains planned with no published launch date. Iruka will not advertise shipment insurance or coverage limits until a carrier and insurance policy are active.

After carrier handoff, delivery exceptions follow the shipping terms and carrier claim process published at launch.
