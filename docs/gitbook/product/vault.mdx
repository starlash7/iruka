> **Status: In validation** - The account inventory interface is live, but
> commercial intake, storage, and redemption operations are not live.

The Iruka Vault is intended to connect one digital inventory record to one
verified physical card. A UI record, testnet pull, image, or certificate alone
does not prove custody.

## Custody State Model

| State | Required condition |
| --- | --- |
| `Intake` | Physical item received and assigned an `inventoryId` |
| `Authentication` | Identity, images, condition, and certificate evidence under review |
| `Verified` | Physical item and evidence match the acceptance policy |
| `Vaulted` | Storage location, custody, ownership, and redemption records agree |
| `Listed` | Vaulted item locked for an active marketplace listing |
| `Redemption locked` | Listing removed and item reserved for physical fulfillment |
| `Redeemed` | Custody released through the documented shipment or collection process |

Failed authentication, damaged intake, unresolved ownership, or missing storage
records prevent the transition to `Vaulted`.

## Required Custody Record

Each vaulted item must connect:

- Unique `inventoryId`
- Exact front and back photographs
- Authentication or grading evidence
- Intake and verification timestamps
- Tamper-evident seal or slab identifiers
- Controlled storage location reference
- Current owner reference
- Listing lock, if any
- Redemption eligibility and status
- Audit history for every state change

Sensitive storage locations and operator notes remain private. The public record
shows only the evidence needed to understand status and eligibility.

## Ownership And Custody

Physical custody and digital ownership are separate responsibilities. Iruka's
custody ledger records where the item is and whether it can move. Planned
ERC-721 ownership will record who controls the eligible vaulted item.

Production actions must fail closed:

- An unverified item cannot become `Vaulted`.
- A non-vaulted item cannot be listed.
- A listed item cannot enter shipping.
- A redemption-locked item cannot be sold.
- A redeemed item cannot return to inventory without a new intake.

## Operations To Publish Before Launch

Before commercial custody opens, Iruka will publish the legal custodian,
storage controls, access policy, incident handling, inventory reconciliation,
fees, supported items, and customer support process. No coverage, storage fee,
or custody service level should be inferred until those terms are published.
