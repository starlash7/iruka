The Iruka Marketplace is designed for fixed-price trading of verified physical
cards already held in the Vault. It is not an open listing board for
unverified seller images.

## Listing Eligibility

A card can be listed only when:

- Its `custodyStatus` is `Vaulted`.
- Authentication and item photographs are complete.
- The ownership record references the same `inventoryId`.
- Redemption is allowed and no redemption request is active.
- The item is not already listed or transfer-locked.
- Required seller and regional checks are complete.

Creating a listing locks the item against redemption. Cancelling or completing
the listing releases or transfers that lock through one recorded state change.

## Listing Record

Each listing is intended to show:

- Exact card identity and inventory ID
- Front and back item photographs
- Grader, grade, and certificate verification when applicable
- Vault and redemption eligibility
- Seller's fixed price and payment currency
- Dated estimated-value evidence, when available
- Listing and update timestamps

Estimated value is not a recommended price or guarantee. Collectors choose
whether to list, buy, or wait.

## Planned Settlement

The production target is an atomic flow:

1. Buyer approves the supported payment asset.
2. Marketplace contract validates listing and ownership.
3. Payment settles according to published fee rules.
4. Ownership transfers to the buyer.
5. The same physical item remains in custody under its existing `inventoryId`.
6. Listing status closes and the Vault record updates.

The current GIWA test contract does not perform this settlement and does not
mint ownership tokens.

## Launch Disclosures

Before trading becomes live, Iruka will publish:

- Supported GIWA network and canonical payment asset
- Contract addresses and verified source
- Platform, creator, payment, and withdrawal fees
- Gas responsibility and transaction-failure handling
- Listing cancellation and dispute rules
- Identity, age, and regional restrictions
- Indexing, outage, and recovery procedures

No fee percentage or settlement guarantee applies until these terms and
contracts are public.
