> **Status: Planned** - Current listings demonstrate the interface and do not settle real payments or ownership transfers.

The Iruka Marketplace is for fixed-price sales of authenticated cards that remain in the Vault. Auctions, offers, item swaps, and user consignment are not part of the initial release.

## Listing Eligibility

A card can be listed only when:

- Its physical custody and BRG records are valid.
- The connected wallet owns its ERC-721.
- The card is not already listed, sold, or locked for redemption.
- The seller has completed the required account and age checks.

Listings show actual inventory photos, the identifying group or member name, BRG grade and certificate, inventory ID, USDC price, valuation evidence, custody status, and redemption eligibility.

## Settlement

1. The seller sets a fixed USDC price and the card is locked for sale.
2. The buyer confirms payment from a GIWA wallet.
3. Marketplace settlement transfers USDC and the ERC-721 together.
4. Iruka updates the custody ledger without moving the physical card.
5. The seller receives USDC in the connected wallet.

If settlement fails, neither payment nor ownership should move partially.

## Fees and Gas

The initial platform fee is 0% for buyers and sellers. Iruka plans to sponsor gas for Marketplace actions. External wallet transfers remain subject to the network fee shown before confirmation.

Marketplace payments activate only after canonical USDC support on GIWA is verified. Contract addresses will be published before trading opens.

Sellers may edit or cancel an unsold listing. A listed card must be delisted before physical redemption can be requested.
